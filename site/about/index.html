About Greg

Greg Tysick did not leave operations to chase AI. He brought decades of operational judgment into the work of deploying AI inside real businesses.

Nearly three decades of work — from accounting and controls through business building, analytics, automation, and now practical AI systems — have followed one consistent principle: turn complexity into something people can actually use.

Today Greg builds agent operating systems, structured context surfaces, and governed workflows that make AI useful inside real business operations. The tools changed. The work stayed the same: reliable information, reliable operations, and systems that reduce friction rather than add it.

How Greg thinks

  • Systems over tasks. The workflow around the work matters as much as the work itself.
  • Measure, then improve. Define the rule, observe what happens, iterate.
  • Practical over theoretical. A system that works in a real business beats an elegant one that doesn't.
  • Judgment compounds. Operational instincts from decades of real work make AI deployment safer, not riskier.
