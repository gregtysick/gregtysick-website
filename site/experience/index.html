01 / 1990s–2008Accounting & operational discipline

The Ledger

Reliable decisions start with reliable information.

My early work centred on accounting, financial controls, payroll, budgeting and reporting. Accuracy mattered, but so did the operational decisions behind the numbers.

I learned to look beyond a completed reconciliation or report: where did the information originate, how did it move, who relied on it, and what would make the process more dependable next time?

02 / 2009–2012Working across small businesses

The Practice

A recurring problem is usually a systems problem.

Working with multiple small businesses revealed familiar friction in different forms: delayed information, duplicated entry, difficult handoffs and processes that depended too heavily on one person's memory.

The shift was from completing each task to understanding the workflow around it. Good documentation, repeatable processes and clearer responsibilities made the work easier to sustain.

03 / 2012–2017High Speed Canada · business operations

The Network

As a business grows, every system becomes connected.

At High Speed Canada, my work intersected finance and operations in a telecommunications environment: billing, payroll, CRM, multi-province activity, people and processes.

A change in one part of the business could affect several others. The challenge became keeping information and operations aligned as the organization grew more interconnected.

04 / 2017–2023VitaCeuticals & Group Vita

The Builder

Build the process, not just the report.

Product businesses brought together inventory, product costing, vendors, ecommerce, reporting, marketing and team coordination. The work crossed departmental lines and required a clearer view of the business as a whole.

I became increasingly interested in designing the systems connecting those activities: making work visible, reducing unnecessary manual steps and helping teams operate from more consistent information.

05 / 2022–presentSystems · analytics · measurement

The Models

Measure the signal. Define the rule. Observe. Improve.

My work evolved toward integrated systems, dashboards, KPIs and programmatic decision-making — connecting operational data to clear measures, useful reporting and repeatable decisions.

The value is in making performance observable and decisions understandable: define what matters, measure it consistently, and improve the process as the evidence changes.

06 / CurrentModern workflows · AI-native adoption

Modern Workflows

Modern technology should simplify operations, not create another layer of complexity.

I help businesses transition toward AI-native ways of working: modern workflow architecture, capable agents, structured context, approvals and reliable handoffs.

The emphasis is on practical adoption and forward-deployed operations — making the technology fit real teams, preserving human judgment, and building systems people can trust.
